How to Use the Fee Portal
  1. Click the "Go to Fee Portal" button above
  2. Select the term you want to view fee records for
  3. Select your name from the student list
  4. Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format
  5. Click "View Fee Report" to see your fee details
  6. Optional: Click "Download Report" to save a copy
If you can't find your name, please ensure you've selected the correct class and term.
